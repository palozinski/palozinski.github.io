RISE support

RISE is an alarm clock with wake-up missions. If something does not work as you expect, start here. If that does not help, write to palozinski@gmail.com and say which phone and iOS version you use.

My alarm did not ring

  • Open Settings in RISE and press Test alarm. It rings in a minute or two, so you can hear that your phone really does it.
  • A set alarm is not a guarantee. A phone that is switched off or has a dead battery cannot ring, and Focus or Do Not Disturb settings can change what you hear.
  • On iPhone, RISE needs the Alarms permission (Settings > RISE > Alarms). The first alarm you set asks for it.
  • RISE needs iOS 26.1 or later, because it uses the system's own alarm feature.

How do wake-up missions work on iPhone?

  • The system Stop button always works. That is Apple's rule for alarms, so a mission can never trap you.
  • After you stop it, the alarm comes back every minute, up to 8 times, until you finish the mission in the app. Holding the emergency exit for five seconds ends it too.
  • Missions are Math, Memory, Shake and scanning a QR code you register. You can combine up to five steps.

How does Snooze work?

You choose the interval (1, 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes) and how many times. On iPhone every snooze rings as its own alarm until you turn the alarm off in the app. "Unlimited" means up to 12.

RISE Pro

  • Pro is one purchase, no subscription. It adds the Memory, Shake and QR missions, multi-step missions and the Hard level. Alarms, Stop, Snooze and the Math mission at Easy and Medium are always free.
  • Restore a purchase: Settings > RISE Pro > Restore purchase.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

My data

RISE has no account and no server. Your alarms and history stay on your phone, and deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.
